### Questions to Ask a Roommate About Shared Expenses

"We'll just split everything" lasts until the first uneven bill.

Six questions to settle before you move in together: equal rent or by room, who pays each provider, which supplies are shared, what changes when a guest stays, and what happens when a share is late. Worked scenarios included.

### How to Track Shared Household Expenses

Paid. Owed. Repaid. Three columns, one balance.

Our fictional month follows two housemates through rent, groceries, bills and two repayments, and shows why a repayment should never be split like a purchase. Print the free two-page ledger or use the CSV.

### First Apartment Essentials: What to Buy Now or Later

Your first apartment list doesn't all have to be bought before move-in day.

Sort everything into three columns: need now, borrow or share, postpone. In our fictional example, a $725 list becomes $225 before the keys, with the rest moved to later paydays.

Free checklist, no signup.
