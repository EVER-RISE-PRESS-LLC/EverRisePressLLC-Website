## Audience and topic selection

Focus on readers interested in everyday money choices, first-time independent living and thoughtful discussion of money memoirs. Practical money content uses sourced educational examples; the author is not presented as a financial adviser. Search phrases below are editorial targets, **not verified search-volume or keyword-difficulty estimates**. No purchased keyword dataset is available. Before drafting each batch, inspect the actual results for its targets, retain the distinct intent and adjust weak phrasing within that day's production work.

The existing payday story, weekly calendar, book club guide, advice reading note, housing comparison, memoir/workbook comparison and moving-cost inventory retain their current URLs and central queries. New articles must answer a narrower or different task. If an intended article proves duplicative, replace its brief with a distinct reader question in the same cluster; do not inflate the article count with near duplicates.

## Publication and distribution requirements

1. Draft from primary evidence and the book manuscript where relevant. Keep fictional examples labelled and arithmetic correct; never invent author quotations or experiences. Money claims require appropriate official sources and careful scope.
2. Give each article one clear question, a distinct title/description, a substantive original example, meaningful headings and accessible graphics where they explain something. Disclose the publisher's relationship when comparing its own book with others.
3. Publish media and articles through the scoped EverRise API, preserving reviewed snapshots. Use Chrome only for visual/print checks or services without suitable access, always the everrisepressdev@gmail.com profile.
4. At release, verify the canonical, indexability, schema, image rendering, sitemap and related links. Add Article/Breadcrumb markup where applicable; do not promise FAQ rich results or AI citations. Use one useful topic hub, two relevant incoming links and a reader-appropriate next step per article.
5. Keep printable content private to the reader; avoid collecting financial details. Count resource downloads separately from sample unlocks and retailer clicks where supported by consent-aware analytics.
6. Submit an updated sitemap through available supported access if needed. Search Console inspection is not an indexing request. Use legitimate request-indexing access only when available; never the unrelated Indexing API for ordinary blog posts.
7. Each article receives a finished social post. Six selected topics receive carousel scripts. Twelve personalized pitches offer interviews, discussions or useful public resources. The owner authorizes sending and social posting separately; shipping review copies is excluded.

## Research basis and sourcing starting points

These sources support the content approach and factual research; they do not validate traffic estimates for all 24 phrases.

- [Google: helpful, reliable content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): useful original contributions, clear responsibility and reader-first production.
- [CFPB Your Money, Your Goals toolkit](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tool/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/): reference for bill timing, spending categories and cash-flow education. Create original resources rather than rebranding their tools.
