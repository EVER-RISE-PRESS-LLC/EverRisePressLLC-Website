Fictional example only · USD · two housemates, Jordan and Sam

Record who paid, each person’s share, and any repayment on separate lines. The last column is the running amount Sam owes Jordan. Figures are invented, not typical costs or a recommended split.

Rule: when Jordan pays, add Sam’s share. When Sam pays, subtract Jordan’s share. A repayment from Sam to Jordan subtracts the amount repaid.

Check: shared spending $1,570 = Jordan’s shares $905 + Sam’s shares $665. Jordan paid $1,440 and Sam paid $130. Sam’s repayments of $480 + $55 = $535 close the gap, so the balance ends at $0.
