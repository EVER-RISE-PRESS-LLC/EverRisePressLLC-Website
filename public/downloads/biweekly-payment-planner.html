Check: $400 + $2,400 − $1,650 = $1,150. The lowest balance is $100, before the first paycheck. A $300 payment on December 2 leaves $850 before the December 4 paycheck, with other costs still to record.

A third-paycheck month still needs a continuing ledger. Example: $200 opening + $1,000 on days 1, 15 and 29 − $700 on day 3 − $900 on day 18 = $1,600. A $900 payment on day 2 of the next month leaves $700 before the next paycheck on day 12. Confirm actual payroll dates.
