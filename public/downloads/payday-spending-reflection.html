Private payday spending reflection

Use this sheet to note payments for seven days. Keep bills and food costs beside the purchases you choose. Your notes stay with you; there is nothing to send to us.

Start date Next expected payday

Use one entry for each payment. Seven entries are provided; print extra copies if you need more. Describe the context in your own words. A necessary cost can be unplanned. These labels describe spending; they do not grade it. No name or account number is needed.
