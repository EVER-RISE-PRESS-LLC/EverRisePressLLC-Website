Opening + new income − payments = projected ending balance. A negative result marks an unfunded payment. It does not mean a bank will let the payment clear or provide an overdraft.

A positive week can still hide an earlier gap. Continue on page 2 to check each date. Do not count the weekly summary again in the dated ledger.

If essential costs exceed available income, changing dates does not create more money. A provider must agree to a date change; check fees and the actual collection date.
